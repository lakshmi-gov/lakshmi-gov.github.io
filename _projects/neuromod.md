A natural outcome of building models whose internal representations mirror biological mechanisms is that they can serve as functional blueprints for neuroprosthetics and other neurotechnologies. I’ve had the opportunity to design AI pipelines that guide *in vivo* neuromodulation in a fully automated, data-driven, and rapid way.
