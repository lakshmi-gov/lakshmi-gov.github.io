I am an ICoN Postdoctoral Associate at the McGovern Institute for Brain Research at MIT working with [Josh McDermott](https://mcgovern.mit.edu/profile/josh-mcdermott/) and [Ila Fiete](https://mcgovern.mit.edu/profile/ila-fiete/). 

I received my PhD in 2022 from Brown University in Cognitive Science advised by [Thomas Serre](https://serre.lab.brown.edu/). During that time I was a Carney Graduate Fellow as part of the Robert J. & Nancy D. Carney Institute for Brain Science, a [DARPA Riser](https://forward.darpa.mil/Risers), and received the [Presidential Award for Excellence in Teaching](https://graduateschool.brown.edu/news/2021-04-20/lakshmi-govindarajan). My dissertation was on [Attractor dynamics in large-scale recurrent neural networks]() [[Video]()].

Before graduate school, I worked in industry in a Statistics and Machine Learning group at A*STAR (Singapore) following undergraduate degrees in Computer Science and Biophysics from National University of Singapore.
