🚨 New paper out in *Nature Biomedical Engineering* <br>

We report on AI-guided neuromodulation in humans with spinal cord injury, showing how machine learning can personalize electrical stimulation of the spinal cord to restore ability to move limbs, receive sensory feedback. <br>

Incredibly proud of this interdisciplinary team and the years of work that made this possible. <br>

[Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41551-026-01627-5) + [Press release](https://www.brown.edu/news/2026-03-11/spinal-cord-stimulation) now available.
