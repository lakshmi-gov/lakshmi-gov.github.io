Our paper on [AI-guided neuromodulation](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41551-026-01627-5) in humans with spinal cord injury is now published in *Nature Biomedical Engineering* (associated press release can be found [here](https://www.brown.edu/news/2026-03-11/spinal-cord-stimulation)). Congrats to the entire team on this major milestone!

🚨 New paper out in *Nature Biomedical Engineering*

We report AI-guided neuromodulation in humans with spinal cord injury, showing how machine learning can personalize electrical stimulation of the spinal cord to restore ability to move limbs, receive sensory feedback.

Incredibly proud of this interdisciplinary team and the years of work that made this possible.

[Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41551-026-01627-5) + [Press release](https://www.brown.edu/news/2026-03-11/spinal-cord-stimulation) now available.
